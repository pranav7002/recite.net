- **p50 8,721 ms / max 21,123 ms** (all 11, including the misheard MSE retry).
- **p50 8,764 ms / max 21,123 ms** (10 valid questions, dropping the misheard retry) — barely moves the median; use either.
- **vs. the script's `stream` row: p50 8,172 ms, p95 20,364 ms.** The manual p50 is ~550-590ms higher (recording stop, upload, and playback start — exactly the gap the script can't see, and a small one). The manual max lands almost on top of the script's p95, so the script's tail isn't a `say`-vs-human-voice artifact; it's real.
- **2 of 11 answers were wrong, and both are worth separating**: one was a genuine STT mishearing (a single word, "MSE" → "MSC", not a model/retrieval failure — resolved instantly on re-asking), the other was a real refusal on a legitimate question ("data leakage" / train-test gap) with no transcription issue, i.e. an actual retrieval or grounding gap, not a speech problem. Two different failure classes; don't conflate them in the writeup.
